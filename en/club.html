Aba’a.
The spirit of welcome.

“Come in, you are home.” — a lodge conceived as a home, in step with the forest and the ocean.

Our story

A home born
of a conviction.

Aba’a, in Gabon’s Fang culture, means the guardhouse — the palaver hut at the heart of the village where travellers are welcomed, where words and fire are shared. Mvoé is peace, the greeting offered to those who arrive.

Together, the name says one simple, whole thing: the house of welcome and peace. An exceptional lodge that combines authentic African hospitality with contemporary refinement, at the edge of Akanda’s coastal forest.
