Our story

A home born
of a conviction.

Aba’a, in Gabon’s Fang culture, means the guardhouse — the palaver hut at the heart of the village where travellers are welcomed, where words and fire are shared. Mvoé is peace, the greeting offered to those who arrive.

Together, the name says one simple, whole thing: the house of welcome and peace. Aba’a Mvoé Lodge is a high-end Business Lounge in La Sablière, Libreville, with a cigar cellar, dining area, spirits bar, pool area and private event hire.
